What We Treat

Clinical Services

Explore our core internal medicine services by selecting a category below.

Preventative Health & Annual Exams

Preventing illness before it starts is the foundation of internal medicine. We evaluate your personal and family medical history to build a practical screening schedule.

  • Comprehensive annual physical examinations
  • Blood pressure & cholesterol screenings
  • Age-appropriate cancer screening referrals
  • Adult vaccinations & booster updates

Ongoing Chronic Illness Oversight

Managing multiple medications or long-term health conditions requires careful monitoring and clear communication between you and your doctor.

  • Type 1 & Type 2 diabetes management
  • Hypertension (high blood pressure) control
  • Asthma, COPD & respiratory care
  • Thyroid & metabolic disorder monitoring

In-Office Diagnostics & Lab Coordination

We evaluate acute adult symptoms promptly and coordinate any necessary blood work or diagnostic imaging with local hospital facilities.

  • Electrocardiograms (EKG)
  • Routine & fasting blood panel orders
  • Pre-operative medical clearances
  • Medication reviews & refill management
