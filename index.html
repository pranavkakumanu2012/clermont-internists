What we help with

Our practice is focused entirely on adults. Here is what you can expect from a visit.

Routine physicals and preventive care
Annual exams, blood pressure monitoring, cholesterol evaluations and immunizations.
Proactive health screenings
Routine lab work, physicals and age-specific risk assessments, including cancer risk screening recommendations.
Long-term condition management
Structured treatment and prescription management for type 2 diabetes, high blood pressure, thyroid disorders, asthma and cardiovascular risk factors.
Chronic illness oversight
Tailored care programs for hypertension, diabetes, cholesterol, arthritis and cardiovascular health.
Outpatient diagnostics and lab work
In-office evaluations, electrocardiograms (EKG) and routine blood work ordering.
Specialist referrals
Direct referral coordination with specialized medical centers when you need more care.

New patients and insurance

We are accepting new patients. We accept Medicare and Medicaid. Call us to confirm that we take your specific plan before your first visit.

Please bring to your first visit

  • A photo ID
  • Your insurance card
  • A list of your medications and doses
  • Records from your previous doctor, if you have them
