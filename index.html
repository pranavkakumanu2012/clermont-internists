About Our Practice

What is Clermont Internists Associates?

We are a specialized medical practice focused on Internal Medicine—the medical specialty dedicated to the prevention, diagnosis, and treatment of adult diseases.

Unlike general family practices that treat patients of all ages, our internists focus exclusively on adults, complex medical conditions, long-term health management, and routine preventative care.

Proactive & Preventative Healthcare

Preventative medicine is the cornerstone of internal care. We help identify potential health risks before they turn into serious medical issues through routine screenings and wellness evaluations.

Expert Long-Term Disease Management

Managing chronic conditions requires continuity and close doctor-patient collaboration. We specialize in coordinating care for ongoing health concerns.

Diagnostic & Clinical Evaluation

When you feel unwell or develop new symptoms, our internal medicine specialists perform thorough medical evaluations to determine the exact cause.
