About Our Practice

What is Clermont Internists Associates?

We are a specialized medical practice focused on Internal Medicine—the medical specialty dedicated to the prevention, diagnosis, and treatment of adult diseases.

Unlike general family practices that treat patients of all ages, our internists focus exclusively on adults, complex medical conditions, long-term health management, and routine preventative care.
