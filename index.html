About Our Medical Practice

At Clermont Internists Associates, our board-certified physicians specialize in adult health care. Internal medicine focuses on the prevention, diagnosis, and treatment of medical conditions in adults from young adulthood through the senior years.

We work directly with our patients to establish personalized care plans, emphasize routine screenings, and manage both acute medical issues and chronic long-term health needs.

Clinical Services

We provide a complete range of internal medicine and outpatient primary care services, including:

  • Preventative Care & Screenings: Annual physical exams, routine blood work, cardiovascular health evaluations, and age-appropriate cancer screenings.
  • Chronic Condition Management: Long-term treatment plans for hypertension, diabetes, high cholesterol, asthma, arthritis, and thyroid disorders.
  • Acute Illness Care: Prompt evaluation and medical treatment for minor illnesses, infections, and common conditions.
  • Diagnostic Testing: Electrocardiograms (EKG), blood glucose monitoring, routine lab test ordering, and specialist referrals.
